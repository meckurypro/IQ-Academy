IQ Academy

Physical AI Skills Academy — Programme & Package Framework

1. What IQ Academy Is

IQ Academy is a practical, physical learning academy focused on helping people understand technology, solve problems, create, and build.

The academy is not designed around collecting certificates or teaching people how to operate individual software tools.

Its core philosophy is:

«Learn the concept. Understand the problem. Use the tools. Create something. Build something. Solve something.»

Students will receive certificates upon successful completion of their programmes, but the certificate is evidence of demonstrated learning—not the primary product.

The real product is capability.

A student should leave IQ Academy having created or built something tangible and having developed the ability to apply what they learned to real problems.

---

2. Physical Academy Model

IQ Academy will initially operate physically without requiring the company to own or permanently lease its own educational facility.

Rather than carrying the overhead of a dedicated building, IQ Academy can operate from existing:

- Extra-moral/tutorial centres
- JAMB/WAEC tutorial centres
- Educational centres
- Suitable classrooms and training facilities

IQ Academy independently recruits and registers its students.

The host centre primarily provides physical infrastructure.

This means:

IQ Academy owns and controls

- The IQ Academy brand
- Student relationships
- Student registration
- Curriculum
- Course structure
- Instructors
- Teaching methodology
- Marketing
- Tuition collection
- Certificates
- Student records
- Programme quality
- Learning outcomes

The host centre provides

- Classroom space
- Access to agreed facilities
- Electricity/utilities where agreed
- Basic facility access and maintenance
- Agreed classroom capacity
- Security/facility access as applicable

The preferred commercial arrangement is a fixed monthly facility fee, rather than revenue sharing.

The host centre is therefore being paid for the use of its infrastructure—not for IQ Academy's success.

---

3. Why the Physical Host-Centre Model

The model allows IQ Academy to start lean.

Instead of committing significant capital to:

- rent,
- renovation,
- furniture,
- utilities,
- security,
- facility maintenance,

IQ Academy can use infrastructure that already exists.

This also makes the academy highly scalable.

A future IQ Academy branch does not necessarily require IQ Academy to acquire another building.

It can simply establish a programme at another suitable educational centre.

This creates a potential model of:

«Perch. Operate. Build demand. Expand.»

The academy can maintain a presence at a centre for as long as the arrangement remains commercially and operationally sensible.

If a host centre eventually becomes unreasonable about pricing or terms, IQ Academy can move because the students, brand and programme belong to IQ Academy, not the facility provider.

---

4. Host-Centre Commercial Structure

The preferred arrangement is a fixed monthly facility fee.

For example, an agreement could provide:

- A defined contract term, potentially 12 months
- A fixed monthly facility fee during the initial term
- No fee calculated from IQ Academy's student numbers
- No percentage of IQ Academy tuition
- No percentage of IQ Academy revenue
- No requirement for the centre to collect student tuition
- Clearly defined classroom access
- Clearly defined days and hours
- Agreed maximum/classroom capacity
- Permission for IQ Academy signage
- Clear renewal terms
- Any change in facility fee requiring mutual agreement during renewal

The central principle is:

«If IQ Academy grows from 10 students to 100 students, the host centre does not automatically become entitled to more money.»

The centre provides capacity and infrastructure. IQ Academy carries the business risk and earns the business reward.

---

5. Physical Learning Schedule

IQ Academy's programmes are designed around regular physical sessions rather than occasional seminars.

The current operating concept is approximately:

«3 sessions per week»

Sessions may be approximately 2–3 hours depending on the programme and practical requirements.

The exact timetable can therefore be adapted to the host centre and the needs of each cohort.

The emphasis is on hands-on learning, not simply classroom lectures.

Students should spend significant time:

- Practising
- Experimenting
- Creating
- Building
- Testing
- Iterating
- Solving practical problems

6. The IQ Academy Course Catalogue

IQ Academy currently has 10 core courses/skills:

- Embeddings
- Prompting
- AI capabilities and limitations
- The modern AI ecosystem
- AI tools and providers
- AI in business
- Identifying opportunities for AI
- Understanding how AI systems are used to solve problems

This is not intended to be purely theoretical.

The goal is to give students the mental model necessary to understand everything else they encounter at IQ Academy.

---

7. The Learning Philosophy

The 10 courses are not intended to function as 10 unrelated classes.

They form a learning ecosystem.

The fundamental philosophy is:

«Understand → Design → Build → Integrate → Automate → Architect»

Students should progressively move from understanding technology to applying it to increasingly complex problems.

This means IQ Academy can impose sensible prerequisites without forcing every student through the entire catalogue.

---

8. Prerequisite Structure

AI Foundations is the common starting point.

Every student should understand the fundamentals of AI before moving into the specialised pathways.

This is particularly important because IQ Academy is an AI-native academy.

Even Product Design students need to understand AI because AI is part of the way they will research, think, prototype and scaffold solutions.

Therefore:

«AI Foundations → everything else»

But after Foundations, different skills branch into different pathways.

---

9. Product-Building Progression

One major pathway is:

«AI Foundations → Product Design → Web/Mobile Development → AI Solutions & Integration»

However, these do not necessarily need to be sold as one giant package.

They can be divided into sequential bundles.

Bundle 1

AI Foundations + Product Design

The student learns:

«Understand AI → understand problems → research → design solutions.»

Then the student can progress.

Bundle 2

Web Development + AI Solutions & Integration

The student now learns:

«Build the product → make the product intelligent.»

Alternatively, the student could take:

Mobile App Development + AI Solutions & Integration

The student therefore has a choice of product-building direction.

This is important:

The prerequisite relationship does not mean every prerequisite must be repeated or bundled together.

Students can complete one stage and return for the next.

---

10. Automation & Agent Progression

Another pathway is:

«AI Foundations → Workflow Automation → AI Agents & Automation»

The educational logic is straightforward.

First:

«How do I identify and automate a workflow?»

Then:

«How do I use AI to perform tasks inside that workflow?»

This allows the student to progress from conventional automation into intelligent automation.

---

11. Creative Progression

The creative pathway can be structured as:

«AI Foundations → AI Content Creation → Cinematic Filmmaking»

A student can begin by learning how AI can be used creatively.

They can then progress into more sophisticated cinematic production.

Possible progression:

Bundle 1

AI Foundations + AI Content Creation

Bundle 2

Cinematic Filmmaking

The student isn't starting over. The second programme builds on the first.

---

12. AI Systems Progression

The advanced AI pathway can develop as:

«AI Foundations → AI Solutions & Integration → AI Agents & Automation → AI Architecture Essentials»

However, AI Agents can also be reached through Workflow Automation.

This makes AI Agents a kind of convergence point between automation and AI product development.

A student could therefore arrive at Agents through:

Workflow Automation → AI Agents

or:

Web/Mobile Development → AI Solutions & Integration → AI Agents

Then students who want deeper systems knowledge can progress toward:

AI Architecture Essentials

14. Pricing Philosophy

The pricing is based on bundle size and learning duration, not on the perceived value of an individual software tool.

2-course programme

6 weeks — ₦50,000

3-course programme

10 weeks — ₦70,000

The ₦70,000 programme provides:

- One additional course
- Four additional weeks
- More practical work
- More time to build
- A deeper skill combination

This makes the longer programme more valuable without making the shorter programme unattractive.

---

15. Students Can Progress Through Multiple Programmes

A key part of the IQ Academy model is that a student does not have to complete everything at once.

For example:

Stage 1

AI Foundations + Product Design

6 weeks — ₦50,000

The student learns to understand problems and design products.

Stage 2

Web Development + AI Solutions & Integration

6 weeks — ₦50,000

The student learns to build the product and integrate AI.

Stage 3

AI Agents & Automation

A subsequent programme can take the student further into intelligent systems.

Stage 4

AI Architecture Essentials

The student can eventually progress into advanced AI systems architecture.

The result is a continuing educational relationship between IQ Academy and its students.

---

16. The Certificate

Certificates will be issued upon successful completion of a programme.

However, IQ Academy should avoid making the certificate itself the centre of the proposition.

The certificate should document the actual learning pathway completed.

For example:

«IQ Academy Certificate
AI Foundations + Product Design & UI/UX»

or:

«IQ Academy Certificate
AI Foundations + Product Design & UI/UX + AI-Powered Web Development»

This is more meaningful than simply issuing a generic:

«“Certificate in Artificial Intelligence.”»

The certificate represents what the student actually studied and completed.

---

17. Practical Completion Standard

Completion should ideally require more than attendance.

Students should demonstrate that they can apply what they learned.

Depending on the pathway, this could involve:

- A completed creative project
- A content portfolio
- A short film
- An automated workflow
- A product design
- A functional website
- A deployed web application
- A mobile application
- An AI-integrated application
- An AI agent
- An AI system architecture/project

The principle is:

«Don't just tell us you learned it. Show us what you built with it.»

18. The IQ Academy Difference

IQ Academy is therefore not positioned as a traditional training centre where students attend classes, learn software, write an examination and collect certificates.

Its physical programme is built around practical capability.

The academy teaches students to:

Understand problems.

Think critically.

Understand technology.

Use AI.

Design solutions.

Create.

Build.

Integrate.

Automate.

Solve real problems.

Certificates are part of the programme, but the real outcome is what the student becomes capable of doing.

The ultimate IQ Academy philosophy can be summarized as:

«We don't teach skills for certificates. We teach skills that attack problems.»

And the learning journey reflects that:

«Understand → Design → Build → Integrate → Automate → Architect.»
